Before you start

CardioLens 3D is an educational, decision-support prototype built for the Multimodal AI Hackathon 2026.

  • It estimates the probability of coronary artery disease and of ≥50% stenosis in the LAD, LCX and RCA from routine clinical, ECG, lab and echo data.
  • It was trained on a single public cohort of 303 patients. Its accuracy is limited, especially for LCX and RCA (see the Model card).
  • The 3D heart is a schematic. Colours show vessel-level probabilities, not the location of a lesion.
  • It must not be used to diagnose, treat or rule out disease.
